The bundled LilexNerdFontMono-*.ttf files are Lilex (see LICENSE.txt) patched with Nerd Fonts
(their name tables read "Nerd Fonts 3.5.1"). The patcher's own license and the license of the Nerd
Fonts project's fonts are in LICENSE-nerd-fonts.txt.

The patch adds glyphs from the icon sets listed below. The section that follows is copied
verbatim from Nerd Fonts' license audit:
https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/blob/v3.5.1/license-audit.md
(tag v3.5.1, commit b894ea7803af6aade63d60a4381e006098ec9c4d).
